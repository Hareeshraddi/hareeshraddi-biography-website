Chapter 01

Where My Story Begins

Every engineer has an origin story. Mine starts with simple curiosity.

I have always been the kind of person who asks “how does this work?” — and then refuses to stop until I find out. Long before I wrote my first line of code, I was fascinated by how small ideas could turn into things people use every day.

Growing up, technology felt like magic to me — phones, websites, apps that seemed to know what you needed. But instead of just using them, I wanted to understand them. That curiosity slowly turned into a habit: observing, questioning, experimenting, and learning.

When I joined Computer Science Engineering at SKSVMACET, it felt like I had finally found the right room. Suddenly, the “magic” had names — logic, algorithms, networks, interfaces — and I could start learning the craft behind it.

“I don't just want to use technology.
I want to build it.”

Today, as a 5th-semester student, I describe myself simply: a curious, self-motivated builder who learns by doing — and who is just getting started.

Chapter 02

Why I Chose Computer Science

It wasn't a random choice. It was the only choice that made sense.

01

Ideas become real here

In computer science, an idea in the morning can become something people touch by evening. That speed — from thought to working software — still amazes me, and it's why I love building for the web.

02

Problems are puzzles

I genuinely enjoy debugging. Every error message is a clue, every bug a small mystery. CS rewards the exact kind of patient, logical thinking I naturally enjoy.

03

Impact at scale

A well-built tool can help thousands of people — students, patients, small businesses. I want to build software that is not just clever, but genuinely useful and trustworthy.

“I chose computer science because it lets me be logical and creative at the same time — an engineer with an artist's curiosity.”
